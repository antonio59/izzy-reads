## Managing Your Books

### Adding a New Book

1. Go to **My Books** (`/books`)
2. Click the **"Add Book"** button
3. Search for your book by title or author
4. Click on the book you want
5. Choose where to put it:
   - **Finished** - If you already read it
   - **Currently Reading** - If you're reading it now
   - **Wishlist** - If you want to read it later

### Book Statuses Explained

| Status | What It Means | What You Can Do |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Finished** | You've read this book! | Add a rating (1-5 stars), write a review, record who gave it to you as a gift |
| **Currently Reading** | You're in the middle of it! | Click "I Finished!" when you're done |
| **Wishlist** | Books you want to read | Click "Start Reading" when you begin |

### Rating Your Books

After you finish a book, you can:
- Give it **1-5 stars** (5 = AMAZING, 1 = not your favorite)
- Write a **review** telling everyone what you thought
- Mark if it was a **gift** from someone special (like Grandma or your best friend!)

### Filtering Your Books

You can filter your books to see:
- All books from a specific person who gave them as gifts
- Books by genre
- Search by title or author

## Writing Poetry

### How to Write a Poem

1. Go to **Create** (`/create`)
2. Click on **"New Poem"**
3. Choose a template:

| Template | What It Is |
|----------|------------|
| **Haiku** | A Japanese poem: 5 syllables, then 7, then 5 |
| **Acrostic** | The first letter of each line spells a word! |
| **Free Verse** | No rules - just write from your heart! |
| **Rhyming** | Lines that rhyme together |
| **Shape Poem** | Words arranged to make a picture |
| **Upload** | Upload a photo of a handwritten poem |

### Making Your Poem Pretty

You can customize your poem with:
- **Backgrounds**: Sunset, Ocean, Forest, Lavender, Golden, Classic
- **Fonts**: Elegant, Modern, Playful
- **Alignment**: Left, Center, Right
- **Bold** and *Italic* text
- **Emojis** to express yourself!
- **GIFs** for extra fun!

## Writing Blog Posts

### How to Write a Blog Post

1. Go to **Create** (`/create`)
2. Click **"New Blog Post"**
3. Write your title
4. Write your content
5. Add emojis if you want!
6. Tag your post (like "book review", "reading tips", etc.)
7. Link it to a specific book if it's about one
8. Save as **Draft** (only you can see) or **Publish** (everyone can see!)

## The Wishlist & Book Suggestions

### Your Wishlist

Your wishlist shows books you want to read. It's public, so family members know what to get you!

### Getting Book Suggestions

Here's the cool part - visitors can suggest books for you!

1. They click **"Suggest a Book"** on your wishlist
2. They search for a book and tell you why you'd love it
3. You see the suggestion in your dashboard
4. You can **Approve** (adds to wishlist), **Decline**, or **Delete** it
